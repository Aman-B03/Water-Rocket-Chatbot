Code of Cooperation (Ghada) 

Deadlines: All members will complete their tasks on time. If someone can’t, they tell the team at least 24 hours ahead.
Decision method: Consensus. Major choices affect everyone’s grade, so every member should be heard. It gives the best decisions and makes sure everyone agrees and supports the outcome. 
Meetings: We meet weekly. Agendas are prepared beforehand and minutes are recorded and shared within 24 hours.
Roles: Every member has a role and contributes fairly to the rocket, report, chatbot, and presentation.
Communication: We use WhatsApp and reply within 12-24 hours.
Respect: We listen to all ideas, criticize ideas not people, and involve the instructor if a conflict can’t be resolved.
Safety: We follow all project safety rules, and safety is never up for a vote.
Accountability: Repeated missed deadlines or meetings lead to a warning, then a discussion with the instructor.
Agreement: All members have agreed. This code is kept in a shared google document  so everyone can access it.
